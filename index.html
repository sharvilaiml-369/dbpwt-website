WHO WE ARE

A values-led approach to public welfare.

DBPWT is constituted as an Eco-Socio-Spiritual Trust. Its stated purpose is to promote a culture of peace, value education and sustainable development in India.

The Trust brings together the Fundamental Duties of Indian citizens, the Commitment to Global Peace, the Sustainable Development Goals, Natural Farming, Guru-Shishya Parampara, and Indian Culture & Heritage.
