OUR FOUNDATIONS

Three reference frameworks. One direction of service.

The Trust deed expressly incorporates Fundamental Duties, the Commitment to Global Peace and the Sustainable Development Goals into its stated constitutional foundation.

51-A

APPENDIX A

Fundamental Duties of Indian Citizens

The deed identifies Article 51-A of the Constitution of India as a constitutional foundation for the Trust's work. The listed duties include respect for the Constitution and national symbols; preserving sovereignty, unity and integrity; promoting harmony; preserving cultural heritage; protecting the environment; developing scientific temper, humanism and a spirit of enquiry; safeguarding public property and rejecting violence; striving for excellence; and providing educational opportunities for children aged 6–14.

The website presents these themes in summary form; the full legal text should be read in the Trust's governing documents.

2000

APPENDIX B

Commitment to Global Peace

The deed records a Commitment to Global Peace signed during the Millennium World Peace Summit of Religious & Spiritual Leaders at the United Nations in New York, 29–31 August 2000. It describes commitments around human dignity, justice, peace, nonviolent conflict resolution, religious freedom and reconciliation, education and healthcare, sustainable livelihoods, poverty eradication, ecological care, reforestation, and the practice of inner peace.

SDGs

APPENDIX C

Sustainable Development Goals

The deed incorporates the Sustainable Development Goals adopted by the United Nations in 2015 as a reference framework for sustainable development. DBPWT's objectives connect this global framework with practical work in education, agriculture, culture, community welfare and environmental responsibility.

1 · No Poverty2 · Zero Hunger3 · Good Health & Well-being4 · Quality Education5 · Gender Equality6 · Clean Water & Sanitation7 · Affordable & Clean Energy8 · Decent Work & Economic Growth9 · Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure10 · Reduced Inequalities

The scanned deed pages available for this build visibly list the first ten SDGs; the website therefore avoids inventing text for the remaining goals.

THE THREAD BETWEEN THEM

Duty → values → action → sustainable peace.

DBPWT's public narrative can be understood as a progression from individual and civic responsibility, through education and human values, into practical work that supports people, nature and peaceful coexistence.
