ABOUT DBPWT

A public charitable trust rooted in peace, values and service.

Constituted at Lucknow in September 2026, Dharma Bharti Public Welfare Trust is designed to bring together social welfare, education, culture, agriculture, sustainability and peace.

THE PURPOSE

A culture of peace, value education and sustainable development.

The Trust's deed describes DBPWT as an “Eco-Socio-Spiritual Trust” established for the welfare of society and the nation through peace, value education and sustainable development.

Its beneficiaries are stated as all citizens, with particular attention to farmers, women, youth and children of India.

OUR STORY

From an existing body of work to DBPWT.

Earlier work

Dharma Bharathi National Institute of Peace, Value-Education & Sustainable Development

The covering letter accompanying the deed says the work had previously been carried out under DBNI, Lucknow, a project of Navasrusthi International Trust, formed by Acharyashri Dr Sachidananda Bharathi.

2026

Dharma Bharti Public Welfare Trust

DBPWT is constituted to carry forward and integrate stated activities around peace, value education, sustainable development, natural farming, education, culture and community welfare.

Legacy

Swargiya Major Markandey Singh Smarak Sewa Sansthan

The deed records an institute at Jogam, Deoria, established in 2001, with value education, Yoga, sports and games, and wrestling competitions among its activities since 2002. The deed provides for integrating these activities with DBPWT's aims.
