OUR WORK

Turning a values framework into practical action.

The Trust deed identifies a set of interconnected areas. This page presents them as a coherent public-facing programme architecture.

01

PEACE & VALUE EDUCATION

Education that develops values, responsibility and peace.

DBPWT seeks to promote peace and value education through the Guru-Shishya Parampara and through activities associated with the Dharma Bharathi National Institute of Peace, Value-Education & Sustainable Development.

PeaceValuesHumanismEnquiryEducation
02

NATURAL FARMING

Rishi-Krishi Parampara.

The deed explicitly calls for the promotion of Natural Farming through the Rishi-Krishi Parampara, expressed in the phrase “Krishi Karo-Rishi Bano”.

Agriculture is presented not only as production, but as a pathway connecting livelihood, nature, knowledge and values.
03

INDIAN CULTURE & HERITAGE

Bharathiya Sanskriti, Sanskar and 18 branches of knowledge.

DBPWT aims to promote Indian Culture & Heritage through Bharathiya Sanskriti and Sanskar, based on the Ashtadasha Vidyasthanani — the 18 branches of knowledge referenced in the deed.

04

SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT

Local responsibility within a global framework.

The Trust incorporates the Sustainable Development Goals as one of its foundations and links sustainable development with environmental responsibility, social welfare and human dignity.

PeoplePlanetProsperityPeacePartnership
05

COMMUNITY WELFARE

Serving citizens, with a focus on farmers, women, youth and children.

The deed names all citizens as beneficiaries and gives particular emphasis to farmers, women, youth and children. It also provides for mobilisation of resources and communication through print, electronic and social media in pursuit of the Trust's objectives.

A CONNECTED MODEL

One mission, many forms of service.

The Trust's objectives are deliberately broad enough to connect education, agriculture, culture, sustainability and peace. The common thread is the stated aim of building a culture of peace, value education and sustainable development.
