HOW IT WORKS

Governance by the deed.

General Body

11 members. Meets at least once each financial year and approves the Plan of Action and Budget, Annual Report and Audited Accounts.

Board of Trustees

Five office bearers. Implements the General Body-approved Plan of Action and manages the Trust's affairs within the deed.

Financial year

The deed specifies the Government of India financial year.

Irrevocability

The deed declares the Trust irrevocable, with dissolution subject to the procedure specified in the deed.

INSPIRATION & GUIDANCE

Acharyashri Dr. Sachidananda Bharathi

The deed names Acharyashri Dr. Sachidananda Bharathi as Inspiration & Guidance and describes his work in peace, education and related institutions. The deed also lists Baba Dr. Hardeo Singhji, Padma Shree Dr. Vidya Vindu Singh and Padma Shree Dr. Ram Chet Chaudhary as patrons.
